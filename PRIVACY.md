# Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy covers Touch Type for Windows and Mac (the desktop app from this repository) and Touch Type for iPhone, iPad and Mac (the Apple app).

## The short version

- Touch Type has no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs.
- Your data is stored on your device. The developer doesn't collect any of it and can't see it.
- The Apple app can sync your data through your own private iCloud. You can turn that off.
- The app only goes online to download things you ask for, and to sync with iCloud in the Apple app.

## What the app stores on your device

- Profiles: the names you give them.
- Settings: theme, fonts and game options.
- Results: speed, accuracy, which keys you missed and the words you mistyped, for tests, practice and games.
- Replays: the keys you pressed in each round and when, so rounds can be replayed and analysed.
- Songs you add to Tiles: the audio file, its name, its beat, and any lyrics you add or that were in the file.
- Game progress: scores, unlocked animals and similar.
- Optional models you download in Settings → Models. These contain no personal data.

The desktop app keeps this in its own data folder on your computer. The Apple app keeps it in its own storage on your device, which is included in your device's backups like other app data.

## iCloud sync (Apple app only)

The Apple app can keep your devices in step through iCloud, using a private database in your own iCloud account. Apple stores it, and only your Apple ID can read it. The developer has no access to it.

- **What syncs:** your profiles, settings, results and game progress, and the songs you add (their audio, name and lyrics), plus deletions. Replays stay on the device where they were recorded.
- **Storage:** songs you add use your iCloud storage.
- **Turn it off:** Settings → Data → iCloud sync.
- **Delete what's in iCloud:** Settings → Data → Delete from iCloud. This removes everything Touch Type stored in your iCloud. It doesn't touch the data on your devices.

## When the app goes online

- **Models:** when you click Download in Settings → Models, the app downloads the model from this repository's releases on GitHub. Each file is checked against its published checksum before it's used.
- **Updates (desktop app):** the app checks GitHub for a new version only when you click Check for updates in Settings → Updates, or, on Windows, when you turn on automatic updates. Automatic updates are off by default. The Apple app is updated by the App Store.
- **iCloud sync (Apple app):** as described above, when it's on.
- **Links:** links to web pages open in your web browser.

When the app downloads something from GitHub, GitHub receives the request like any other download (for example your IP address), under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The app sends nothing else.

## Deleting your data

- **One profile's data:** Settings → Data → Delete this profile's data.
- **A profile:** Settings → Profiles → Delete.
- **Everything on this device:** Settings → Data → Delete all data on this device. This removes every profile, setting, result, replay and added song. The desktop app can also remove the downloaded models.
- **Your iCloud copy (Apple app):** Settings → Data → Delete from iCloud.
- **Uninstalling:** deleting the Apple app removes its data from the device. Uninstalling the desktop app can leave its data folder behind, so use Delete all data on this device first.

## Children

Profiles can carry any name, including a child's. Like everything else, profile names stay on your device and, in the Apple app with sync on, in your own private iCloud.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
